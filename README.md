# AURA SKIN — AI Product Campaign Case Study

A complete AI-generated marketing campaign for a fictional luxury skincare brand, built as a digital marketing portfolio project.

---

## What's Inside

This project showcases a full end-to-end campaign created entirely with AI tools — from product photography to ad copy, email marketing, and social media content.

---

## Campaign Assets

### 🖼️ AI Product Photography
Four hero visuals generated with Leonardo.ai using custom prompts.

See the prompts and results in `/visuals` folder.

| Shot | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Hero Shot | Product on marble, golden hour light |
| Texture Shot | Golden serum drop, black background |
| Lifestyle Shot | Model with glowing skin holding product |
| Flat Lay | Product with roses, gold rings, silk fabric |

---

### 🎬 Product Video / Reel
15-second AI product video created with Kling AI, edited in CapCut.

W
Scenes:
- 0 to 3 sec — Hero shot with cinematic light movement
- 3 to 7 sec — Golden drop slow motion macro
- 7 to 11 sec — Model glowing skin close-up
- 11 to 15 sec — Brand outro with CTA

---

### ✍️ Instagram Captions
Three ready-to-publish captions for different post formats.

See `/copy/instagram-captions.md`

---

### 📢 Meta Ad
Full Meta advertisement copy including primary text, headline, description and CTA recommendation.

See `/copy/meta-ad.md`

---

### 📧 Launch Email
Complete product launch email with subject line, preview text, body copy, offer and testimonials.

See `/copy/launch-email.md`

---

### 📱 Social Media Content Plan
Two-week content calendar for Instagram and TikTok including hooks, formats, visuals and goals.

See `/content-plan/social-plan.md`
